## Which ISO 27001 technical controls do we implement in Microsoft 365?

_(Intro paragraph shown above the heading:)_

ISO 27001 is based on a complete Information Security Management System: policy, risk assessment, people and physical controls, internal audit, and an independent certification audit. Our scope is narrower and technical: we implement and harden the Annex A Technological controls (section 8 of ISO/IEC 27001:2022) in Microsoft 365, on which your ISMS relies.

_(Below the heading:)_

We focus on the Microsoft 365 technical controls that support your ISMS, with clear evidence and explicit approval for each change.

### 01 — Access Control & Identity
Conditional Access, MFA and privileged role restrictions in Entra ID.

### 02 — Cryptography
Encryption at rest and in transit on Exchange, SharePoint and OneDrive.

### 03 — Logging & Monitoring
Unified audit log configuration and retention for evidence and detection.

### 04 — Secure Configuration
Hardened tenant, endpoint and application settings.

### 05 — Network & Application Security
Defender policies, conditional access for apps and secure collaboration settings.

### 06 — Backup & Data Protection
Resilient, independent recovery for Microsoft 365 data.
