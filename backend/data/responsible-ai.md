## Our Responsible AI Principles

Deploy IQ’s Responsible AI approach is grounded in practical principles shaped by real-world security operations, risk management, and regulatory expectations.

### Human Accountability

AI supports decision-making; humans remain responsible for outcomes.

Security decisions, risk acceptance, and change approvals are always owned by accountable roles, never delegated to AI.

### Reliability and Safety

AI is used only where outcomes can be evaluated and controlled.

All AI-assisted outputs are reviewed and validated before influencing security changes or deployment decisions.

### Transparency and Traceability

AI-assisted actions are visible, traceable, and reviewable within delivery workflows.

Where AI influences analysis or recommendations, that influence can be examined, understood, and audited.

### Security and Privacy by Design

AI operates within strict security and data-handling boundaries.

Protecting system integrity and customer environments is a foundational design constraint, not a secondary consideration.

### Augmentation, Not Replacement

AI augments professional judgement rather than replacing it.

Human experience, context, and responsibility remain central to all security outcomes.

## How Deploy IQ Uses AI Responsibly

Deploy IQ leverages AI for its speed, broad knowledge, and ability to evaluate multiple dimensions of risk simultaneously. Our approach ensures AI accelerates security delivery while human accountability remains central.

### AI in Practice

Deploy IQ applies AI to:

- Support assessment and analysis across complex environments
- Bring cross-domain security knowledge into evaluations
- Identify patterns, dependencies, and risk trade-offs
- Evaluate technical, operational, and governance risks in parallel
- Validate outcomes against defined expectations and maturity targets

_(Callout)_ AI is never used to independently authorise, deploy, or scale security-critical changes.

### Evaluated and Controlled Execution

AI-assisted activity is continuously evaluated throughout the delivery lifecycle. Deploy IQ enforces:

- Validation before scaling any AI-influenced change
- Review of both security effectiveness and operational impact
- Explicit acceptance or rejection of risk by human decision-makers
- Evidence generation to support audit, assurance, and compliance

_(Callout)_ No AI-assisted change progresses without meeting clearly defined evaluation criteria, ensuring safe, accountable, and trustworthy security delivery.

### Human Oversight and Governance

Responsible AI at Deploy IQ is enforced through operational controls, not policy statements alone. This includes:

- Defined approval points for all risk-bearing actions
- Explicit evaluation checkpoints at every stage of delivery
- The ability to pause, adjust, or reverse actions at any time

_(Callout)_ AI accelerates delivery. Governance ensures control.
