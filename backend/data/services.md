## ISO/IEC 27001 Technical Controls in Microsoft 365

ISO 27001 is based on a complete Information Security Management System: policy, risk assessment, people and physical controls, internal audit, and an independent certification audit. Our scope is narrower and technical: we implement and harden the Annex A Technological controls (section 8 of ISO/IEC 27001:2022) in Microsoft 365, on which your ISMS relies.

All technical control changes within Microsoft 365 are reviewed and approved by your team before they occur. We implement; your ISMS owner owns the policy and risk decisions.

## CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark Assessment

The CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark, published by the Center for Internet Security (CIS), is a consensus-built standard covering identity, endpoints, email and collaboration. We assess and configure your tenant against it as your technical partner, with every change reviewed and approved by you before it is made.

### 1 Identity (Entra)

Sign-in and access security across your Microsoft 365 environment.

### 2 Microsoft Defender

Threat protection across mail, endpoints and cloud apps.

### 3 Exchange, SharePoint & Teams

Safe collaboration and sharing defaults.

### 4 Admin Center, Intune & Purview

Tenant-wide governance and data controls.

We implement the highest-impact, lowest-effort fixes first and do not make any change without your approval.
