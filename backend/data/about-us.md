### We implement; independent bodies certify.

DeployIQ assesses, hardens and documents your Microsoft 365 environment. We provide technical evidence to your team and, where required, your independent assessor or accredited certification body.

For ISO 27001, our scope is the Annex A Technological controls within Microsoft 365. Your ISMS owner retains responsibility for policy, risk treatment, organisational, people and physical controls. The independent certification audit sits with your accredited certification body.

## How Deploy IQ Works

The same governed journey connects onboarding to ongoing improvement, with human approval at every stage:

_(Seven step icons, in order)_

1. Onboard
2. Assess
3. Recommend
4. Approve
5. Apply
6. Validate
7. Improve

_(Callout box)_ Your team confirms the connection scope and target standard, reviews the remediation plan and approves each recommendation before agents apply it. Changes run within safeguards with rollback and audit logging. Outcomes are validated before completion, and every new improvement follows the same approval process.
