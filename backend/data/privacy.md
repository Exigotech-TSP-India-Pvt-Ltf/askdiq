## 1 Scope

_(The number is shown in a badge beside the heading.)_

This policy applies to information collected through:

- the Deploy IQ website
- forms and chatbot interactions
- email, phone, and meeting communications
- pre-engagement assessments
- delivery of security and compliance services

It covers both personal information and other business or environment data provided during service engagements.

## 2 Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Provided Directly

We may collect:

- name, email address, phone number, company, role
- enquiry details provided through forms or chatbot
- information shared during workshops, discovery sessions, or assessments
- documents and artefacts needed to understand an organisation's environment

We do not require unnecessary sensitive personal information.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Our website may collect:

- IP address, device information, browser type
- pages visited and interaction patterns
- performance and analytics data

This information is used to maintain and improve the website.

## 3 How We Use Information

Deploy IQ uses information to:

- respond to enquiries
- assess environment context
- scope and deliver services
- communicate with authorised contacts
- meet operational, legal, or audit obligations
- maintain business records

De-identified or aggregated data may be used internally for service improvement.

## 4 Data Handling & Governance

Deploy IQ applies a controlled, human-in-the-loop approach to all aspects of service delivery. Information provided to Deploy IQ is handled according to these principles:

### 4.1 Governance Principles

- Explicit ownership and approval points
- Traceable actions and controlled execution
- Security controls aligned to operational risk
- Transparency in how information is used and protected

### 4.2 How We Handle Customer Environment Data

We may process information relating to:

- identity, device, application, or network context
- operational posture
- compliance or maturity indicators
- customer priorities and constraints

This information is used solely to support assessment, prioritisation, and delivery activities defined in scope.

### 4.3 Human Oversight and AI Boundaries

Deploy IQ uses AI-assisted analysis only within governed boundaries. AI does not:

- deploy or approve security-critical changes
- make binding decisions
- operate without human review

All actions with risk impact are performed or approved by qualified personnel.

### 4.4 Access Controls

- Role-based, least-privilege access
- Controlled handling of operational artefacts
- Defined retention and removal practices

## 5 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

### 5.1 What Cookies Do

Cookies help the Deploy IQ website function, maintain security, and measure performance.

### 5.2 Types of Cookies Used

- **Strictly necessary:** support navigation and secure access
- **Performance & analytics:** provide aggregated usage insights
- **Functional:** enable convenience features
- **Third-party cookies:** from trusted providers supporting analytics or embedded components

### 5.3 Managing Cookies

Users may block or remove cookies through browser settings. Doing so may affect certain features or functionality.

## 6 Sharing of Information

We may share information with:

- authorised Deploy IQ personnel
- trusted operational suppliers (e.g., cloud platforms, communication tools)
- regulators when required by law
- customer-approved third parties

We do not sell personal information under any circumstances.

## 7 Data Locations

## 8 Retention

Information is retained only for the duration needed to deliver services or meet legal obligations. It is then securely archived or disposed of.
