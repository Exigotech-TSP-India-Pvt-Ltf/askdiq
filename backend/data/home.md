## The DeployIQ Journey

Onboard → Assess → Recommend → Approve → Apply → Validate → Improve

Human-in-the-loop applies to every stage below, not only Apply — nothing moves to the next stage without an explicit approval from a person.

Select a stage to explore the journey. _(Mobile/tablet only:)_ Swipe to see all stages.

Stage selector (step 01 selected by default):

- Step 01 — Onboard
- Step 02 — Assess
- Step 03 — Recommend
- Step 04 — Approve & Apply
- Step 05 — Validate
- Step 06 — Improve

_(Detail panel — shows "Step 0X of 06", the stage title, its description and its approval gate.)_

### Onboard
_(Selected: Step 01 of 06 — default)_

Zero-touch onboarding: agents connect to identity, endpoints, and cloud on their own, within a scope you approve upfront. No manual setup; credentials are scoped and encrypted, and the environment is fingerprinted in minutes.

**Approval gate:** Connection scope signed off before anything connects.

### Assess
_(Selected: Step 02 of 06)_

You select the target standard (Essential Eight, CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark v7.0.0, ISO 27001, Zero Trust, Security Baselines). An automated scan against that control set scores current maturity and surfaces every gap.

**Approval gate:** Standard and scope confirmed before the scan runs.

### Recommend
_(Selected: Step 03 of 06)_

AI generates a risk-prioritised remediation plan. Each recommendation states the control, current vs. target state, and business impact — not just “fix this.”

**Approval gate:** The plan is reviewed before anything is queued for action.

### Approve & Apply
_(Selected: Step 04 of 06)_

Approved recommendations only

Nothing executes on its own. A person approves each recommendation individually; only then do agents execute the change under guardrails with rollback, with every action logged as audit evidence.

**Approval gate:** Explicit per-recommendation approval — the hard requirement of the whole journey.

### Validate
_(Selected: Step 05 of 06)_

Outcomes are confirmed, configuration states verified, and alignment to the required standard checked before anything is marked complete.

**Approval gate:** Validation results reviewed and accepted.

### Improve
_(Selected: Step 06 of 06)_

Posture keeps adapting as your business, technology, and risk conditions change, with new recommendations surfaced continuously on ongoing engagements.

**Approval gate:** Each new improvement follows the same approve-before-apply rule.
