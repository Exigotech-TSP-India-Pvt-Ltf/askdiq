## What does a Microsoft 365 Security Baseline Assessment cover?

We evaluate your tenant across five areas that matter most: identity, email, endpoints, endpoint protection and backup. We translate what we find into plain-language recommendations, each with a clear reason behind it.

### 01 — Identity & Access
Sign-in security, multi-factor authentication and admin protection.

### 02 — Email & Collaboration
Anti-spoofing, anti-phishing and safe sharing defaults.

### 03 — Endpoint Management
Uniform and hardened configuration of all endpoints.

### 04 — Endpoint Protection
Threat detection and response coverage.

### 05 — Backup & Recovery
Resilient, independent data protection.
