## What does a CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark assessment cover?

We assess Microsoft 365 configuration across identity, threat protection, collaboration, tenant governance and data controls, then prioritise practical improvements.

### 01 — Identity (Entra)
Sign-in and access security across your Microsoft 365 environment.

### 02 — Microsoft Defender
Threat protection across mail, endpoints and cloud apps.

### 03 — Exchange, SharePoint & Teams
Safe collaboration and sharing defaults.

### 04 — Admin Center, Intune & Purview
Tenant-wide governance and data controls.

## How does the CIS Benchmark assessment work?

1. Step 1 — Onboard
2. Step 2 — Assess
3. Step 3 — Recommend
4. Step 4 — Approve _(highlighted)_
5. Step 5 — Apply
6. Step 6 — Validate
7. Step 7 — Improve

We implement the highest-impact, lowest-effort fixes first and do not make any change without your approval.

## Why work with Deploy IQ on the CIS Microsoft 365 Benchmark?

Microsoft 365 is always changing, and so is the benchmark. We continually compare your tenant to the applicable benchmark to keep your configuration up to date beyond the day of the assessment.
