_(Four cards side by side; each card's title is an H2.)_

### Baseline

_(Label)_ Stage 1

**Know Where You Stand**

A Microsoft 365 security assessment that turns gaps into a clear, prioritized plan.

- **39 controls across 5 focus areas:** Identity & Access, Email & Collaboration, Endpoint Management, Endpoint Protection, and Backup & Recovery
- **Plain-language recommendations:** every finding explains why it matters and how to fix it
- **Mapped to recognized standards:** cross-referenced to the CIS Microsoft 365 Benchmark
- **Specialist walkthrough:** we review the results with you and agree the priorities together

**Compliance:** A first look at where you stand against recognized frameworks, including Essential Eight.

### Foundation

_(Label)_ Stage 2

**Secure What You Already Own**

Get full value from the Microsoft 365 security you already pay for and protect your people.

**Everything in Baseline, plus:**

- **Microsoft 365 hardening:** your baseline applied across identity, email, devices and data
- **Secure sign-in:** multi-factor authentication, Conditional Access and protected admin accounts
- **Device management:** hardened, encrypted and compliant devices managed through Intune
- **Email protection:** anti-phishing, anti-spoofing and email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
- **Business password manager:** a secure, company-managed password vault for every user
- **Security awareness training:** regular staff training and simulated phishing campaigns that build a security-aware team

**Compliance:** Lays the technical groundwork for your Essential Eight journey.

### Strengthen

_(Label)_ Stage 3

**Stop Threats Before They Run**

Advanced identity, threat and data protection for growing and regulated organizations.

**Everything in Foundation, plus:**

- **Risk-based access:** sign-in decisions that adapt to user and session risk
- **Just-in-time admin access:** privileged roles granted only when needed, for as long as needed
- **Extended threat protection:** Microsoft Defender coverage across endpoints, email, identities and cloud apps
- **Data protection and loss prevention:** sensitivity labels, DLP and retention with Microsoft Purview
- **Application allowlisting:** only approved software can run on your devices, so unknown and malicious apps are blocked

**Compliance:** Builds on that groundwork as your Essential Eight journey matures.

### Frontier

_(Label)_ Stage 4

**Secure, AI-Ready and Watched 24/7**

Complete protection for organizations adopting Copilot and AI agents, with round-the-clock monitoring.

**Everything in Strengthen, plus:**

- **24/7 Managed Detection & Response:** round-the-clock threat monitoring, hunting and response by a dedicated security operations team
- **AI-ready data governance:** make sure Copilot and AI agents only reach data people are meant to see
- **AI and agent oversight:** visibility and control over how AI is used across your organization
- **Continuous posture improvement:** regular reviews as your business, technology and risks change

**Compliance:** Choose the framework that matters most to your business, and we align your technical controls to it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

_(Accordion; one item open at a time.)_

### Which Microsoft 365 security package is right for my business?

Start with Baseline. The assessment shows where your Microsoft 365 environment stands, and our specialists recommend the package that fits your risk, size and compliance needs.

### Do your packages help with Essential Eight?

Yes. Our packages are designed with Essential Eight in mind, and Frontier lets you align to the framework that suits your business. We focus on technical controls; formal assessments are carried out by independent assessors.

### Will you make changes without my approval?

No. Every recommendation is reviewed and approved by your team before anything is applied.

### Can I move up to a higher package later?

Yes. Each package builds on the one before, so you can upgrade as your business grows without starting again.

### What is Managed Detection and Response (MDR)?

MDR is monitoring 24/7 of your environment by security analysts who detect, investigate and respond to threats. It's included in our Frontier package.

### Do you help secure Microsoft Copilot and AI agents?

Yes. Frontier includes data governance and oversight controls, so AI tools only reach the information users are meant to see.

### Will this make us Essential Eight compliant or ISO 27001 certified?

Not on its own. We deliver the technical controls and the evidence behind them. Compliance and certification also depend on your policies, processes and people, and are confirmed by an independent assessor or certification body.

### Can I choose which framework you align to?

Yes. In Frontier, you choose the framework that matters most to your business, and we align technical controls to it.
