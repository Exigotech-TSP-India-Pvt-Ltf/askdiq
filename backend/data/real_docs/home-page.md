## __Our Services__

Technical partners, not certifiers\. DeployIQ builds, hardens, and documents your environment against CIS, Essential Eight, ISO 27001, and Zero Trust\. We hand you — and, where one is required, your accredited certification body or assessor — audit evidence that holds up under scrutiny\. We implement; independent bodies certify\.

Continuous, automated hardening assessment across your Microsoft 365 tenant — every control checked against the __CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark v7\.0\.0__ and cross\-mapped to Essential Eight, ISO 27001, and Zero Trust, scored directly from live tenant signals rather than a point\-in\-time manual audit\.

- __Automated Control Verification:__ Controls scored directly from tenant configuration across Identity, Data, Endpoint, Email & Collaboration, and Apps — manual attestation only where a control genuinely can't be checked automatically
- __Live Baseline Score:__ One score that updates as your tenant changes, not a PDF that's out of date the day it's delivered
- __Cross\-Framework Mapping:__ The same scan maps to CIS, Essential Eight, ISO 27001, and Zero Trust controls, so one assessment answers several compliance questions at once
- __Approved\-Only Remediation:__ Every gap comes with a ready\-to\-apply fix — nothing is deployed until a person reviews and approves it

### __2\. Zero Trust__

End\-to\-end management of Essential Eight \(E8\) compliance with intelligent cyber risk prioritisation, governed deployments, and continuous validation\.

- __Gap Analysis:__ Quick identification of cyber risk drivers and Essential Eight maturity gaps

Structured assessment and hardening against the __CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark v7\.0\.0__\.

### __5\. ISO 27001__

We implement the technical side of ISO/IEC 27001 inside your Microsoft 365 environment — the Annex A Technological controls, not the whole ISMS\. Policy authoring, risk treatment ownership, HR/physical controls, and the certification audit itself stay with your ISMS owner and your accredited certification body; we're your technical implementation partner for the Microsoft 365 layer\.

- __Access Control & Identity:__ Conditional Access, MFA, and privileged role restrictions in Entra ID
- __Cryptography:__ Encryption at rest and in transit across Exchange, SharePoint, and OneDrive
- __Logging & Monitoring:__ Unified audit log configuration and retention for evidence and detection
- __Secure Configuration, Network & Application Security:__ Hardened tenant, endpoint, Defender, and collaboration settings
- __Out of scope:__ Organisational, People, and Physical Annex A controls \(policy documents, HR screening, office premises\) — owned by your ISMS owner, outside Microsoft 365

## __The DeployIQ Journey \(Onboard → Assess → Recommend → Approve → Apply → Validate → Improve\)__

__Human\-in\-the\-loop applies to every stage below, not only Apply__ — nothing moves to the next stage without an explicit approval from a person\.

1. __Onboard__ — Zero\-touch onboarding: agents connect to identity, endpoints, and cloud on their own, within a scope you approve upfront\. No manual setup; credentials are scoped and encrypted, and the environment is fingerprinted in minutes\. *Gate: connection scope signed off before anything connects\.*
2. __Assess__ — You select the target standard \(Essential Eight, CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark v7\.0\.0, ISO 27001, Zero Trust, Security Baselines\)\. An automated scan against that control set scores current maturity and surfaces every gap\. *Gate: standard and scope confirmed before the scan runs\.*
3. __Recommend__ — AI generates a risk\-prioritised remediation plan\. Each recommendation states the control, current vs\. target state, and business impact — not just "fix this\." *Gate: the plan is reviewed before anything is queued for action\.*
4. __Approve & Apply \(approved recommendations only\)__ — Nothing executes on its own\. A person approves each recommendation individually; only then do agents execute the change under guardrails with rollback, with every action logged as audit evidence\. *Gate: explicit per\-recommendation approval — the hard requirement of the whole journey\.*
5. __Validate__ — Outcomes are confirmed, configuration states verified, and alignment to the required standard checked before anything is marked complete\. *Gate: validation results reviewed and accepted\.*
6. __Improve__ — Posture keeps adapting as your business, technology, and risk conditions change, with new recommendations surfaced continuously on ongoing engagements\. *Gate: each new improvement follows the same approve\-before\-apply rule\.*
