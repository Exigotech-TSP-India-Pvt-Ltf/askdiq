# CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark Assessment

Configure Microsoft 365 to the industry's most widely adopted hardening standard.

The CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark, published by the Center for Internet Security (CIS), is a consensus-built standard covering identity, endpoints, email, and collaboration. We assess and configure your tenant against it as your technical partner, with every change reviewed and approved by you before it is made.

- **CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark v7.0.0**
- **160 recommendations**
- **Covers 8 areas**

## What Does a CIS Microsoft 365 Foundations Benchmark Assessment Cover?

- **Identity (Entra):** The largest and highest-impact area, covering sign-in and access security.
- **Microsoft Defender:** Threat protection across mail, endpoints, and cloud apps.
- **Exchange, SharePoint & Teams:** Safe collaboration and sharing defaults.
- **Admin Center, Intune, Purview:** Tenant-wide governance and data controls.

## How Does the CIS Benchmark Assessment Work?

**Journey:** Onboard → Assess → Recommend → Approve → Apply → Validate → Improve

We implement the highest-impact, lowest-effort fixes first and don't make any change without your approval.
