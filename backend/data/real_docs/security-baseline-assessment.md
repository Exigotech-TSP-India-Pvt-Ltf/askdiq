## What Does a Microsoft 365 Security Baseline Assessment Cover?

We evaluate your tenant across five areas that matter most: identity, email, endpoints, endpoint protection, and backup, and translate what we find into plain-language recommendations, each with a clear reason behind it.

- **Identity & Access:** Sign-in security, multi-factor authentication, and admin protection.
- **Email & Collaboration:** Anti-spoofing, anti-phishing, and safe sharing defaults.
- **Endpoint Management:** Uniform and hardened configuration of all endpoints.
- **Endpoint Protection:** Threat detection and response coverage.
- **Backup & Recovery:** Resilient, independent data protection.
