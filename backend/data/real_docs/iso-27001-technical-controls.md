# ISO/IEC 27001 Technical Controls in Microsoft 365

We apply the technical aspects of ISO 27001 within your Microsoft 365 environment.

The ISO 27001 standard is based on a complete Information Security Management System — policy, risk assessment, people and physical controls, internal audit, and an independent certification audit. We focus on the scope that is narrower and technical: we implement and harden the Annex A Technological controls (section 8 of ISO/IEC 27001:2022) in Microsoft 365, on which your ISMS relies.

- **Annex A Technological controls**
- **Microsoft 365 scope only**
- **100% your approval, every change**

## Which ISO 27001 Technical Controls Do We Implement in Microsoft 365?

- **Access Control & Identity:** Conditional Access, MFA, and privileged role restrictions in Entra ID.
- **Cryptography:** Encryption at rest and in transit on Exchange, SharePoint, and OneDrive.
- **Logging & Monitoring:** Unified audit log configuration and retention for evidence and detection.
- **Secure Configuration:** Hardened tenant, endpoint, and application settings.
- **Network & Application Security:** Defender policies, conditional access for apps, and secure collaboration settings.
- **Backup & Data Protection:** Resilient, independent recovery for Microsoft 365 data.

## How Does the ISO 27001 Technical Implementation Work?

**Journey:** Onboard → Assess → Recommend → Approve → Apply → Validate → Improve

All technical control changes within Microsoft 365 are reviewed and approved by your team before they occur — we implement, your ISMS owner owns the policy and risk decisions.
