# **About Us**

Security delivery often slows down under manual effort and scattered tools. DeployIQ was created to bring structure, speed, and governance to this work.

We combine secure, governed AI with expert oversight to help organisations move from intention to validated outcomes with clarity and confidence. As environments expand and risks evolve, teams need a system that helps them focus on what matters, act safely, and maintain momentum. DeployIQ provides that system—shortening analysis, accelerating delivery, and ensuring accountability throughout.

DeployIQ is powered by **Exigo Tech**, a global IT solutions provider headquartered in Australia with more than a decade of experience supporting organisations as they modernise, secure, and scale.

## **Our Purpose**

Our purpose is to make security delivery faster, safer, and more effective.
Using secure, governed AI tools, we reduce manual effort, surface the right priorities, and guide organisations toward measurable and durable security improvements. The focus is always on precision and clarity—never automation without oversight.

## **What We Deliver**

- Faster assessment, prioritisation, and decision support using secure AI tooling

- Controlled, human‑approved implementation of security controls

- Continuous validation and monitoring to maintain alignment and effectiveness

- A delivery model built for evolving and scaling environments

## **How DeployIQ Works**

Security delivery follows an orchestrated, continuous loop:

**understand → recommend → implement → validate → monitor → improve**

Governed agents support each step. Humans set intent, define risk boundaries, approve changes, and own outcomes—maintaining efficiency without compromising control.
