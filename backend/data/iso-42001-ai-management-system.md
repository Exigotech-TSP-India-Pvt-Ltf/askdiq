## What does our ISO 42001 technical support cover?

We focus on the technical controls inside your Microsoft 365 environment that support an ISO/IEC 42001 AI management system. Policy, risk ownership and certification stay with your organisation.

### 01 — AI Visibility
Understand which AI tools and agents are in use across your Microsoft 365 environment.

### 02 — Access & Oversight
Controls that help AI reach only the data people are meant to see, with human approval for every change.

### 03 — Data Protection
Classification and protection for the information AI tools work with.

### 04 — Activity Records
Logging of AI activity to support transparency, review and your audit preparation.

_(Scope note box:)_

**Technical controls, not certification.** ISO/IEC 42001 also covers policy, risk assessment and roles, which stay with your organisation. Certification is carried out by an accredited certification body.

## Frequently asked questions

_(Accordion — each answer is revealed when the question is clicked)_

### What is ISO 42001?
ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for managing AI responsibly through an AI management system.

### Will DeployIQ certify us against ISO 42001?
No. We focus on technical controls within your Microsoft 365 environment. Certification is carried out by an accredited certification body.

### Does ISO 42001 relate to Microsoft Copilot?
If your organisation uses AI tools such as Copilot, ISO 42001 can help you govern how they are used. Our focus is the technical controls around that use.
